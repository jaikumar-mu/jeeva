About SWP CAD Tools

SWP CAD Tools is a suite of AutoCAD-based compliance tools developed for Tamil Nadu architects, engineers, and draftsmen to simplify building plan approvals through the Tamil Nadu Single Window Portal (onlineppa.tn.gov.in). All tools are based on TNCDBR 2019 rules including amendments up to July 2026. Developed and maintained from Madurai, Tamil Nadu.
